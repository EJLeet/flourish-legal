Flourish — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Flourish is a personal-finance app published by Sherpa Property Group (“Flourish”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, how we protect it, and the control you have over your data. It reflects how the app actually works today.

What we collect

The financial details you enter (income, savings, debts, goals), your progress through the wealth ladder, and — only if you choose to use the statement importer — the bank statement files you upload and what we read from them: your transactions (with account and card numbers removed), the employer or agency that pays you, and the statement’s dates and balances. We also collect your email address to create and secure your account — though Flourish can be used without creating one, in which case we hold no email address at all. Community activity (streaks, badges) is collected only if you turn sharing on, and shelf prices you type for a scanned product are shared unless you switch that off (see below).

How your money data is protected

Your financial records are private to your account. Every table enforces row-level security, so no other user can read your data. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our backend provider (Supabase). We never sell your data or share it for advertising, and we use no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

The AI statement importer (opt-in)

If you choose to import a bank statement, the file is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) to read its transactions. From them we propose a budget and work out your pay: who pays you, how often, and what that comes to before tax. You see all of it before anything is saved. Your income changes only if you choose “Use as my income”.

We ask for your consent the first time. We keep the transactions it reads, the name of the employer or agency that pays you, and the statement’s period and balances. Account numbers and similar identifiers are removed before anything is stored. Statement contents are never written to our logs, and your statements are not used to train AI models.

Scanning a barcode

On the quick reality cheque, the camera reads a barcode on your device. To find the product and its price, only the barcode number is sent to our servers. They ask Open Food Facts (a free product database) for the product’s name and, when no price is known yet, ask our AI provider (Anthropic) to search Australian retailers’ websites for one — sending the barcode and the product name, nothing about you. We don’t keep a record of what you scanned: we count lookups per account per day to keep the service fair, and keep the names and prices we find, with no link to who asked, so the next person gets them sooner. Looked-up prices are estimates — check the shelf.

Sharing shelf prices

When you type or correct the price of a scanned product, Flourish shares just the barcode, that price and the date, so other members get a price for that product. It’s on by default, and you can switch it off on the quick reality cheque at any time. A price you accept without changing is never shared. Your reports are tied to your account only so you can’t be counted twice and so they’re deleted with your account; no one else sees them — other members only ever see a typical price, once at least three members have reported one. Shared prices are deleted after 120 days.

Who receives your data

Supabase stores your data. Anthropic receives a bank statement only if you use the importer, and a barcode and product name when a price search runs. Open Food Facts receives barcode numbers from our servers. Sentry, our crash-reporting service, receives a report of what went wrong when the app crashes or hits an error — no personal or financial details, and not linked to your account. Anthropic and Sentry are based in the United States and Open Food Facts in France. Each receives only what its job needs.

Data retention

Uploaded statement files are deleted automatically once they have been processed, and within 7 days at the latest. We keep the transactions read from them, with identifiers removed, for your budget and cashflow, until you delete your account. Shared shelf prices are deleted after 120 days, and daily lookup counts after 35 days. The rest of your data is retained for as long as your account exists, and is removed when you delete your account.

Community is anonymous by default

Flourish compares behaviour — streaks, habits, rungs climbed — never your dollar amounts. You appear on leaderboards or the feed only after you turn on “Share my progress” in Settings, and you can turn it off at any time. Pods are private groups you join by invite.

Reporting and blocking

If someone in the community behaves badly, you can report them or their content from the feed and leaderboard, and block them so their activity no longer reaches you. Blocked people are listed under Settings → Community → Blocked users. We review reports within two business days and remove content that breaches our community standards.

Your controls

You can edit or reset your data in the app, turn community sharing and shelf-price sharing on or off, and permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Account → Delete account. Deleting your account removes every owner-scoped record and purges any stored statement files. Turning sharing off removes you from leaderboards and the public feed.

Not financial advice. Flourish provides general guidance and educational tools, not personal financial advice. Figures such as tax and grant rates are indicative — verify them with the relevant authority or a licensed adviser.
