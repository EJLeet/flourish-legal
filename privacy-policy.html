Flourish — Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 June 2026

Flourish is a personal-finance app published by Sherpa Property Group (“Flourish”, “we”, “us”). This policy explains what we collect, how we protect it, and the control you have over your data. It reflects how the app actually works today.

What we collect

The financial details you enter (income, savings, debts, goals), your progress through the wealth ladder, and — only if you choose to use the statement importer — bank statement files you upload. We also collect your email address to create and secure your account. Community activity (streaks, badges) is collected only if you turn sharing on.

How your money data is protected

Your financial records are private to your account. Every table enforces row-level security, so no other user can read your data. Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by our backend provider (Supabase). We never sell your data or share it for advertising, and we use no cross-app or cross-site tracking.

The AI statement importer (opt-in)

If you choose to import a bank statement, the file is sent to our AI provider (Anthropic) solely to categorise transactions into a proposed budget, which you confirm. We ask for your consent the first time. Account numbers and similar identifiers are stripped from transaction descriptions before anything is stored, and statement contents are never written to our logs. Your statements are not used to train AI models.

Data retention

Uploaded statement files are purged automatically by a daily process after they have been processed — we keep only the budget you confirm. The rest of your data is retained for as long as your account exists, and is removed when you delete your account.

Community is anonymous by default

Flourish compares behaviour — streaks, habits, rungs climbed — never your dollar amounts. You appear on leaderboards or the feed only after you turn on “Share my progress” in Settings, and you can turn it off at any time. Pods are private groups you join by invite.

Your controls

You can edit or reset your data in the app, turn community sharing on or off, and permanently delete your account and all associated data from Settings → Account → Delete account. Deleting your account removes every owner-scoped record and purges any stored statement files. Turning sharing off removes you from leaderboards and the public feed.

Not financial advice. Flourish provides general guidance and educational tools, not personal financial advice. Figures such as tax and grant rates are indicative — verify them with the relevant authority or a licensed adviser.
