Common questions

  • How do I delete my account and data? In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data, including any stored statement files.
  • Is the bank-statement importer required? No. It’s entirely optional and opt-in. If you use it, we ask for your consent the first time, and the file is used only to suggest a budget you confirm.
  • Who can see my finances? Only you. Financial data is private to your account. Community features compare habits (streaks, badges), never dollar amounts, and only when you turn sharing on.
  • I didn’t get my confirmation email. Check your spam folder for a message from noreply@wealth.sherpagroup.com.au, then try again. If it still doesn’t arrive, contact us at the address above.
