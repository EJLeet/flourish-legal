Common questions

  • How do I delete my account and data? In the app, go to Settings → Account → Delete account. This permanently removes your account and all associated data, including any stored statement files.
  • Is the bank-statement importer required? No. It’s entirely optional and opt-in. If you use it, we ask for your consent the first time. It suggests a budget and works out your pay, and you see everything before anything is saved. See the Privacy Policy for what it keeps.
  • Who can see my finances? Only you. Financial data is private to your account. Community features compare habits (streaks, badges), never dollar amounts, and only when you turn sharing on.
  • Do I need to create an account? No. Flourish opens straight into the app — no sign-up and no password. Your data lives on your device and syncs privately under an anonymous account we create for you.
  • Someone in the community is behaving badly. You can report a post, a profile or a pod, and block the person, from inside the app. Blocked people are listed under Settings → Community → Blocked users, and their activity stops reaching you straight away. We review reports within two business days — you can also email us at the address above.
